Rules for trainers

  • You can only teach the 21 skills listed on Stezeo. Other skills are not allowed.
  • You cannot set the price. Stezeo sets it automatically by skill tier and level.
  • Upload only your own work. No copied videos, no adult, hateful or illegal content.
  • Your intro video is shown on the Home page and on every student dashboard.
  • Your training videos appear in the student Training page after the student pays.
  • Video files: MP4, WebM or MOV, maximum MB each.
