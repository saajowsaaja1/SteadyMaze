Rules

  1. A competition runs for 2 months. You must complete 8 videos, each with its own deadline.
  2. When a video's deadline passes, that video expires. The next competition starts 2 months later, continuously.
  3. The entry fee must be paid in advance. No payment, no entry.
  4. Users who finish Level 2 of a standard training get one free entry.
  5. If you are removed, you get one chance to book a spot in the following 2-month competition and keep your place.
  6. Someone who completed the 8 videos has already competed; others may take their place.
