Project Competition

100 players — five progressive rounds. Upload a video project to enter. Judges score on Creativity, Presentation, Quality, Frontend; Creativity breaks ties.

Competition Structure

  1. Round 1 — 100 players → Top 50
  2. Round 2 — Top 50 → Top 25
  3. Quarter Final — Top 25 → Top 12
  4. Semi Final — Top 12 → Top 6
  5. Final — Top 6 → Winner

Submissions: student-uploaded video projects (see Upload page). Judges score projects and move qualifiers after each round.

How to Participate

  1. Register or login.
  2. Complete a course or follow the brief for your chosen skill.
  3. Upload a video project (MP4) with a short description and select the skill category.
  4. Winners will be announced on the Leaderboard page each round.
Upload Your Project

Judging Criteria

  • Creativity — main tie-break.
  • Presentation — clarity and storytelling.
  • Quality — production value and polish.
  • Frontend — UI or viewer experience (for web projects).
